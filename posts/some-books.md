Over the past few years, I've revisited the same few ideas, books, authors, and Twitter accounts, and I thought I'd list them down here. A lot of serendipity was involved in discovering them, and I hope this helps someone the same way mentions, recommendations, and citations led me to find their work.

## Nassim Taleb

People don't like him, but his books introduced me to a whole new world and sparked my interest in epistemology, decision theory, probability, and more. I had finished the *Incerto*, his five volume series, with multiple rereads before I turned 19, so he caught me in a formative phase.

## Karl Popper (*The* Philosopher of Science)

Keep an eye out for unfalsifiable statements being taken too seriously.

## Sean McClure (@sean_a_mcclure on Twitter)

He is less famous than the previous two, but I am glad I discovered him on Twitter. Valuable insights that have complexity as their primary theme.

## Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma and Related

Canonical Game Theory thought experiment everyone should know. Tit for tat + forgiveness = better life?

## Peter Thiel (*Zero to One*)

Thiel is probably the only insightful techno-billionaire. Going from zero to one is a fundamentally different change than from 1 to n. It sounds obvious, but may prompt reevaluation of what's worth doing.
